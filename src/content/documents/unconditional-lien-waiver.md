Contractors and suppliers provide an unconditional lien waiver after they have been paid. The form
is usually written in the past tense and may mention that the payment was received and the check
cleared the bank. It immediately releases the right to file a lien, because payment has already
been made.

## How we send it

Our accounts payable team waits a week after sending the check, then sends the unconditional lien
waiver through DocuSign. Signing documents with a DocuSign account is free; if you prefer not to
create one, ask your Project Manager or Project Engineer for the PDF.
