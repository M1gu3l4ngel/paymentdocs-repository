Workers compensation provides medical expenses, lost wages and rehabilitation costs to employees
who are injured or become ill in the course and scope of their job. It also pays death benefits to
the families of employees who are killed on the job.

## How to get a certificate

To get a workers compensation certificate, you first need a workers compensation insurance policy.
The type of insurance depends on the workers compensation laws of your state: you may get it from
a private insurance company, through a state-funded system, or through self-insurance if you have
the resources.

Once you have a policy, ask your insurer for the certificate.
