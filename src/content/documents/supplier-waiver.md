A lien waiver is a formal, legally binding document stating that the claimant (contractor,
subcontractor or supplier) has received or will receive payment for the agreed services or
materials, and therefore waives any right to file a mechanic's lien on the property.

We need a lien waiver from each supplier you have a credit account with. Pick the template by these
two choices, fill it out, and have your supplier date it, sign it and return it to you.

## Conditional or unconditional

- **Conditional:** you owe the supplier money. We pay the supplier directly with a joint check that
  carries both your name and the supplier's, and pay you the remaining balance. Endorse the joint
  check and send it on to your supplier.
- **Unconditional:** you do not owe the supplier money, or you have already paid them directly.

## Progress or final

- **Progress:** you keep using the supplier over several months.
- **Final:** you have finished using the supplier on the project.
