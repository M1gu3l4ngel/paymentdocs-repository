General liability insurance protects against third-party property damage and personal or
advertising injury allegedly caused by you or an employee.

## How to get a certificate

1. **Get the requirements.** Ask the business requesting the certificate for its minimum coverage
   requirements in writing: the liability coverages and limits your company must have to get the
   job. Your agent or insurer needs this information to issue the certificate.
2. **Contact your agent, broker or insurer.** Tell them you need a certificate of insurance. If you
   are not sure your current insurance meets the requirements, ask them to check it, and ask what
   changes are needed and what they cost if it does not.
