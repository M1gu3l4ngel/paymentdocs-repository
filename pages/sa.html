Supplier Affidavit

Send your SA to documents@stoutllc.com

A supplier affidavit, also contractor affidavit, is a form that lists vendors or subcontractors who are still owed money on a project. It is completed and signed by the general contractor or a subcontractor, notarized, and includes a list of vendors or subcontractors and how much each one is owed on the project.

Key Takeaways:

  • Your company name
  • Project that you are working or will be.
  • Name of your supplier
  • Supplier Contact
  • Estimate ($)
  • Name, signature, title and date of signature
Please make sure that you list all your suppliers, if you don't use any supplier you can place N/A.
