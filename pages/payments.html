Stout / Subcontractors

The payment process between Stout and a subcontractor begins once we receive their last document, to return to the previous is that the subcontractor needs several documents for their payment to be processed correctly by our AP team.

Let's review the documents again:

  • W-9 Form
  • General Liability Insurance
  • Workers Compensation Insurance
  • Supplier Affidavit
  • Supplier Waivers
  • Unconditional Lien Waiver

Once our AP team collects all your documents, the process below will begin.

Now let's look at an example of our process:

Suppose that Stout requests the W-9 form on Monday because this is the last document we need, you review and send your document to the Project Manager or Project Engineer on Wednesday, the same Wednesday the document is received by our AP team and processes the payment to the Pay List for the check run that will begin.

On Friday your check would already get into our Check Run and would be cut so on Monday we start printing and reviewing your documents again, on Tuesday they are signed by authorized team members and on Wednesday in the morning we start sending these checks and also are available to pick up in our address 1113 South 500 West Bountiful, UT 84010.

Note that your payment will be added to our process shown above after we received your last document needed by our AP team.
