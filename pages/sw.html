Supplier Waiver

Send your SW to documents@stoutllc.com

A lien waiver is a formal, legally binding document stating that the claimant (contractor, subcontractor, or supplier) has or will received payment for the agreed upon service or materials and therefore waives any rights to file a mechanic’s lien on the property.

We will need a lien waiver from each supplier you have a credit account with. There are 4 different types of supplier lien waiver and their terminology:

  • Conditional Lien Waiver: You owe the supplier money and a Joint Check will be provided by us directly to the supplier and you will be paid the remaining balance; the Joint Check has both your name and your supplier's name on it so you will need to endorse the check and then send it on to your supplier.
  • Unconditional Lien Waiver: You don't owe the supplier money or you have already paid them directly.
  • Progress: You are using the supplier for multiple months, it is ongoing.
  • Final: You are finished using the supplier for the project.

Using the appropiate template, you will need to fill out the info and have your supplier date, sign, and return it to you.

  • Conditional Final
  • Conditional Progress
  • Unconditional Final
  • Unconditional Progress

Key Takeaways:

  • Property Name and Property Location
  • Undersigned's Customer: It should be the subcontractor name
  • Payment Amount: If you owe money to your supplier put the amount, if not put $0
  • Payment Period: If the invoice that you submit were for the February Pay App it means that your supplier waivers should be through February and you can put 02/28/2023
  • Date of signature
  • Company Name: It should be the supplier name
  • By: It should be the supplier signature
  • Its: It should be the title of signer
Note that supplier waivers may vary by state, if you use a waiver created by you include the key points mentioned above.
